4.3. Main results
To demonstrate cross-domain transferability, we exclu-
sively fine-tune the dynamic components (i.e., router and
LoRA adapters) of our SAM2-Hiera-L baseline on DA VIS
2017 dataset. These are directly integrated into SAMU-
RAI [44], SAM2Long [6], and SAM2 [32] without task-
specific retraining. This minimal-cost setup highlights
RDS’s strong generalization, inviting the community to ex-
plore broader dataset fine-tuning.
