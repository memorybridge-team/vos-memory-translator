Effectiveness of the Router.Unlike dense per-block
routing, our single router predicts the active submodel di-
rectly from the input, reducing latency from 17.2 ms to 0.5
ms (Table 4). Moreover, it outperforms static pruning un-
der the same budget (Figure 6), highlighting the need for
adaptive submodels.
Impact of Input on Routing Decisions.Our router fuses
current visual features with a temporal prior (previous
mask). Figure 7 shows that image-only routing struggles
with background distractions, while mask-only routing fails
to adapt to appearance changes. By synergizing spatial at-
tention (where to look) with visual cues (what to see), our
fused approach achieves the best performance.
