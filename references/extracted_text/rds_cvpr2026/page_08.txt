5. Conclusion
In this paper, we aim to reduce the computational burden
of SAM2 for video object segmentation and tracking. Ob-
serving that different samples favor different submodels, we
propose Recurrent Dynamic Submodel. This framework
features a Prediction-Aware Router that adaptively selects
the optimal submodel for each frame by leveraging fused in-
formation from current and previous frames. To further re-
duce adaptation overhead, we introduce Importance-Aware
LoRA, which assigns training weights only to the most
critical components. Extensive experiments demonstrate a
strong performance–speed trade-off, while requiring mini-
mal trainable parameters and training data.
Limitations and Future Work.Our dynamic approach
is currently implemented based on SAM2, but extending it
to other vision foundation models, such as those designed
for streaming video understanding [29, 39] and genera-
tion [19, 22], represents a promising direction. This could
enable efficiency by dynamically allocating computational
resources to targets across continuous frames.
